# Horror craft that worked

* Tension is a number, not a mood: darkness + distance + low battery − lit room, smoothed, driving grain, vignette, heartbeat, room-tone volume and event frequency.
* Events: light stutter on the nearest lamps, a sound from 11–22 m, a whisper word for a blink, a shape at the end of a lit-away corridor that goes when looked at or torched. Never two at once; a 6 s cooldown.
* Set: ruin it — holes with rubble beneath, cracks, fallen tiles with a black hole above, glass shards that catch the torch, wallpaper peeling, furniture thrown, drag marks with hand prints, toys hung by the ankles, plaster statues of the monster, a dead monitor that shows a grin for 0.18 s every ~4 s.
* Story: tapes (VHS glass, typed voice), a PA voice, chapter cards; a second ending for finding everything.
* Fairness: the player must always have a tool (torch, stunner, cubby) and a tell before danger.
