Chomu Game

A first-person zombie survival shooter, built by running Chomugiri's own pipeline rather than by hand. Waves that grow, a hitscan rifle with ammo and reloads, health that regenerates when you back off, and a walled arena with cover to break line of sight. Exported with Godot 4.7.2.

ChomuGame-1.0.apk — 28 MB, arm64, Android 7 and up. It is signed with a debug key, so Android will warn that it came from outside the Play Store; allow the install from your browser and it runs. Left half of the screen moves, right half looks, tap to fire.

How it runs

  • On your device. The workspace is a Next.js app; the Android build carries the whole API surface natively so it works with no server at all.
  • With your keys. You paste them in Settings, they stay in encrypted device storage, and they go only to the provider they belong to.
  • Nothing collected. No accounts, no analytics, no database of ours. See the privacy policy for exactly what leaves the device and where it goes.

The voices

Chomugiri can speak. These are the workspace voices — the same recordings that ship in the app under Settings → Voice. They belong to the app and to this page. They are not baked into anything Chomugiri generates for you: a game it builds ships silent of these, so the thing you make is yours to license as you like.
