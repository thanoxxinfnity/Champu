Privacy

Chomugiri — the autonomous developer workspace.

Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version. Chomugiri has no accounts, no analytics and no server of ours that stores your work. Your chats, files and API keys stay on your device. When you ask it to do something that needs an outside service, it calls that service and nothing else.

What stays on your device

All of it, by default:

  • Chats and generated files — in your browser's IndexedDB, or in the Android app's own storage. Clearing the app's data deletes them, and nothing keeps a copy.
  • API keys — in the Android app these go to the system's encrypted preferences. In a browser they sit in the same local storage as the rest of the workspace.
  • Settings — theme, model choice, custom endpoints.

We do not run a database. There is no account to sign in to, so there is nothing about you for us to hold.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Only what a task needs, and only to the service performing it:

When youWhat is sentTo
Chat with a modelYour message and the conversation so farThe provider you picked — NVIDIA, Pollinations, or an endpoint you added
Generate a texture or imageThe promptNVIDIA or Pollinations
Generate a 3D modelThe promptNVIDIA, Meshy or Tripo, depending on which key you supplied
Deploy a siteThe files being deployed, and your tokenVercel
Use the terminal bridgeThe commandThe machine you pointed it at

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy, and your data is theirs to handle once it arrives. We do not add a middleman: requests go from your device or your own server to them.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because we hold nothing to sell.
  • No training on your prompts or your code.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Internet — to reach the model providers you have configured.
  • Storage — to save the files it generates where you can find them.
  • Notifications — to tell you a long build has finished. Optional; the app works without it.

No camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts.

Voice recordings

The recordings in the app and on this site are ours, and they play from the app. They are deliberately not built into anything Chomugiri generates — a game you export carries none of them, so what you publish is yours.

Children

Chomugiri is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We collect nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, because there are no addresses to notify.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository.
