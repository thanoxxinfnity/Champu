# Website videos — Google Flow prompts (mobile 9:16)

**Only two products now: Chomugiri (the AI) and Chomu Horizon (the racing game).**
(The zombie game is out of the website.)

Nine clips, **10 seconds each**, all **portrait 9:16**. Each starts from a real screenshot so
the video begins from something true and then moves.

How to use in Flow: **Frames to Video** → upload the start frame → paste the prompt → **9:16** →
generate 2–3 takes, keep the best.

## The AI-typing effect (in every clip)

Every clip has a line that **types itself letter by letter, as if an AI is writing it** — a blinking
orange cursor `▍`, handwritten or monospace font, **short simple words only**. The exact words are in each
prompt. The site types the same words in real HTML on top, so a garbled letter in a take never shows.

## The new feature — website names carry their logo

Clips 3 and 4 show it: any website name (claude.com, nvidia.com, vercel.com …) turns into a rounded chip
with **that site's own logo** in front of its name, the same everywhere — in the box you type in, in your
message, in the reply, and in the pages research cites.

## Look to keep consistent

- Chomugiri clips: warm cream paper `#fff7ee`, ink brown `#2c2018`, orange `#ea580c` / `#fb923c` / `#c2410c`,
  hand-drawn wobbly outlines, soft paper grain. The logo chips are soft peach pills with underlined names.
- Chomu Horizon clips: bright, glossy, game-engine look — golden hour, saturated, clean.

Add to the end of every prompt:
*Portrait 9:16. No subtitles, no watermark, no logos other than the orange ">_" mark and the website logos shown on the chips. Only the typed words specified may appear as text.*

## 1 — Hero · `p01_home_light.png`

Portrait. The Chomugiri phone screen on warm cream paper. The glowing orange `>_` mark sits in the center.
Under it a line **types itself letter by letter with a blinking orange cursor: "make me anything"**. As the last
letter lands, hand-drawn orange ink lines burst out of the mark and fold into small paper-cut drawings that float
up and away one after another: an Android phone, a race car, a cube, a website window, a rocket. Camera slowly
pushes in. Soft golden light, floating paper dust, playful and warm.

## 2 — One sentence becomes a plan · `p05_prompt_typed.png`

Portrait screen-recording style, camera locked on the phone app. In the prompt box the sentence **types itself
letter by letter, as if an AI is typing: "build me a racing game"**, cursor blinking. A finger taps the orange run
button. The screen switches to a checklist and seven rows slide in one by one; each row's circle fills orange and
snaps to a check mark in order, a thin orange light running down the edge. Smooth confident UI motion, slight push-in.
Hand-drawn borders, cream background.

## 3 — It researches by itself, and shows where · `p07_research_note.png`

Portrait. The chat screen, then the camera glides into the phone. A line **types itself: "reading live pages..."**
with a blinking cursor. Out of the screen, glowing orange-ink threads fly to a ring of floating paper cards (a code
repository, a docs page, release notes, an encyclopedia page). Tiny glowing orange words lift off the cards and stream
back into the chat. The website names it read pop into the chat one by one as **peach pill chips, each with its own
little logo in front** (a cat-in-a-circle code-host mark, a game-engine mark, a book-globe mark), underlined names.
The line finishes typing **"found it"**. Warm cream and orange, fast and clever.

## 4 — Website names wear their logo · `c01_typing_sites_in_box.png` then `c02_sent_message_with_logos.png`

Portrait. A phone, the prompt box in focus. A list of website names **types itself letter by letter, as an AI would,
one after another: "claude.com  nvidia.com  youtube.com  vercel.com  minecraft.com  google.com"**. The instant each name
is finished, it snaps into a rounded peach pill with **that site's real logo popping in at its left** — a spark-star,
a green eye, a red play button, a black triangle, a green creeper face, a multicolour G — each with a tiny bounce.
The pills fly up into a sent message bubble and settle in a tidy wrapping grid. Soft paper background, orange accents,
crisp and satisfying, like stickers being placed.

## 5 — Light and dark, one thumb · `p02_home_dark.png`

Portrait. A hand holds the phone showing the app in dark mode, orange mark glowing. A thumb taps the sun icon: the whole
screen swipes smoothly into cream light mode, then back. The menu button is tapped and a hand-drawn sidebar slides in and
out. A line **types itself: "works on your phone"**. A tiny paper-craft APK hops out of the screen onto the hand. Soft
window light, shallow depth of field, cozy and tactile.

## 6 — Chomu Horizon: the open road · `h_drift.png` (or `h_low_cam.png`)

Portrait, bright, fast, joyful. A classic blue sports car races down a winding road through green hills at golden hour,
long shadows, a rock cliff on the left. It drifts around a bend kicking up white tyre smoke, blue nitro flames burst,
the camera swoops from behind to the side to a low front shot. Warm sunset light, lens flare, motion blur. A line
**types itself along the bottom, speedometer-style: "drive anywhere"** with a blinking orange cursor. Clean, colorful
game-engine look.

## 7 — Chomu Horizon: three worlds, one key · `h_drive_speed.png` (the portal moment is `h_city.png`)

Portrait. The car drives through a swirling green portal ring and comes out in a different world: first rolling green
hills, then a glowing neon city at dusk, then a red desert canyon at sunset. Each jump is a quick flash of light and a
camera whip. The car keeps its speed across all three. A label **types itself at each jump: "hills" … "city" …
"canyon"**. High energy, saturated colors, motion blur, game-engine look.

## 8 — Chomu Horizon: the garage, and how its cars were made · `h_garage.png`

Portrait. A glossy garage showroom, the car on a turntable. A text prompt **types itself letter by letter in a glowing
bubble: "an old blue sports car"**. The bubble dissolves into a wireframe that sweeps over itself and turns into the
textured car, which slots into the garage turntable. The paint changes colour in a smooth sweep (blue → red → pearl),
underglow switches on. A line **types itself: "cars made by AI"**. Clean showroom lighting, smooth product-reveal motion.

## 9 — Download · `p03_menu_open.png`

Portrait. Two phones float in a column over warm cream — one showing a hand-drawn orange `>_` app, one showing a sunset
race car. Each phone gets a hand-drawn orange arrow that bounces onto a paper-craft "install" button and an APK card pops
into it with a satisfying snap. Confetti of orange paper bits. A line **types itself: "download. install. go."** Camera
slowly pulls back and ends on a calm still frame.
