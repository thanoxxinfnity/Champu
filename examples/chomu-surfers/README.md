# Chomu Surfers

An endless runner, generated by Chomugiri and tested in Godot 4.3 before it was
handed over.

## Opening it on your phone

1. Extract this zip. Internal storage or Downloads, either is fine.
2. Open **Godot Engine 4** (the Android editor) and press **Import**.
3. Point it at the extracted folder and pick `project.godot`.
4. Press **Play** (▶).

## Playing it

| Do this | With touch | With a keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change lane | Swipe left / right | A / D |
| Jump | Swipe up | Space |
| Roll under | Swipe down | S |

You run forward on your own and speed up the longer you last, from 9 m/s to a
cap of 26. Grey blocks you go around or jump; red ones are low, so you roll
under them. Coins are worth 10 points each on top of the distance.

## What is in here

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `project.godot` | Settings. Mobile renderer, portrait, touch input. |
| `main.tscn` | The scene that runs. |
| `game.gd` | One place that decides what a collision means — coin or crash. |
| `player.gd` | Three lanes, jump, roll, and the speed ramp. |
| `track.gd` | The endless track. Segments are recycled, not spawned. |
| `camera.gd` | Chases the runner without riding it. |
| `hud.gd` | Score, coins, and the game-over screen. |
| `character.gd` | Swings the runner's arms and legs from how fast it is going. |
| `hero.glb` | The runner. A rigged, skinned mesh — Godot imports it as a Skeleton3D. |

## Things worth knowing if you change it

- **The track recycles.** Six segments leapfrog each other; nothing is ever
  freed. An endless runner that instantiates a chunk a second fills memory and
  the frame rate falls off a phone within a minute.
- **A segment never blocks all three lanes.** A wall across the track is not a
  challenge, it is a dead end, and the player cannot tell that from a bug.
- **Coins come in lines, not singles.** Scattered one at a time in a random lane
  they are almost never in the lane you are in — five test runs collected one
  coin between them. A line is visible from a distance and gives you a reason to
  pick a lane. `coin_chance` sets how often a trail appears, not each coin.
- **The camera only leans 35% of the way** into your lane. Following you all the
  way looks right until the outside lane leaves the screen and you cannot see
  what you are being steered into. `lean` in `camera.gd` is the dial.
- **Rolling swaps the capsule** for a short one, so a low obstacle really does
  pass over you rather than clipping your head.
- `speed`, `jump_velocity`, `acceleration` and the obstacle/coin chances are all
  exported, so they are editable in the inspector without touching code.
